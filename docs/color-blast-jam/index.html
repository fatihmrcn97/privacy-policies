Who we are

The Game is published by Waow Games. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Waow Games is the data controller for personal data processed through the Game. Contact: help@waow.games.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you.

The Game does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number in the Game.

Information collected automatically

The Game does not automatically collect personal data. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

How we use information

  • To provide and run the Game, save your progress and settings.
  • To detect fraud, cheating and abuse, and to keep the Game secure.
  • To respond to your requests and support messages.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Third-party services

The Game does not include third-party analytics or advertising services.

Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

  • Performance of a contract – to provide the Game.
  • Legitimate interests – security. You can object at any time (see Your rights and choices).
  • Legal obligation – where the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only:

  • if required by law, court order or to protect the rights, property or safety of Waow Games, our players or others;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Policy continues to apply.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends. Game progress stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To exercise these rights email help@waow.games. We will reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Turkey (KVKK). Under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 you may learn whether your data is processed, request information about it, learn the purpose of processing, know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Turkey or abroad, request correction or deletion, object to results arising from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damages. Send requests to help@waow.games; you may also apply to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Deleting your data

You can request deletion of the data associated with the Game at any time, without uninstalling it, by following the steps on our data deletion page or by emailing help@waow.games with the subject "Delete my data – Color Blast Jam". Uninstalling the Game removes the progress stored on your device.

US state privacy rights (including California)

Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete and correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at help@waow.games and we will delete it.

Security

International transfers

Support emails may be processed by our email provider in countries other than yours. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
