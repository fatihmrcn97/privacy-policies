Data deletion request

Color Blast Jam by Waow Games · Privacy Policy

You can ask Waow Games to delete the data associated with your use of Color Blast Jam at any time. You do not need to uninstall the game to make this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to help@waow.games with the subject "Delete my data - Color Blast Jam".
  2. If the game shows a player ID in its settings, include it, so we can find your data. Without an identifier we can only delete data linked to the email address you write from.
  3. We confirm your request by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Support conversations and any other personal data we hold about you.
  • Game progress stored only on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

What may be kept

  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that can no longer identify you.
  • Data we must keep to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a dispute, only for as long as required.
